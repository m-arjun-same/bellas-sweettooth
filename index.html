    (01) ABOUT ME
    
Most of what I bake, you'll never pull off a shelf: the choco drip bento for two, the velvet heart cake, the cupcakes frosted to order — every cake starts as a DM.

    
June bakes from home in Marigold Falls and replies to every message herself, within 24–48 hours. Before you DM, check the “how to order / policies” and “availability” highlights — they hold the open dates and the rules.
